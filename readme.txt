== Description ==

[Proposal Crafter](https://asphaltthemes.com/proposal-crafter) is a powerful WordPress proposal software plugin designed specifically for freelancers and consultants to create professional proposals quickly and send quotes from WordPress without leaving the dashboard. Whether you’re looking for a freelancer proposal or a fast proposal builder WordPress tool, Proposal Crafter simplifies the entire proposal.


**With Proposal Crafter, you can:**
Easily build and customize professional proposals using pre-built templates Send quotes to clients directly from WordPress with just a few clicks. Leveraging a streamlined workflow optimized for freelancers and small agencies.


**Key Features:**  
- 🛠️ **Easy Proposal Builder** – Craft stunning, customizable proposals effortlessly with Gutenberg.  
- 🛢️ **Own your data** – Keep all your proposal data regardless of whether you are a free or pro user.
- ✍ **Multiple Signatures style** – Allow clients to sign proposals online via image upload, type, or both.  
- 🧱 **Gutenberg Blocks** – Easily insert proposal elements anywhere on your site with Drag & Drop Gutenberg blocks.


➡️ **Why Choose Proposal Crafter?**  
- Proposals built specifically for WordPress users.  
- Own your data and never worry about paying just to keep it.
- Mobile-friendly and fully responsive proposals.  
- No coding required – easy drag-and-drop customization.  
- Designed for freelancers & small businesses. 
- Close deals directly within WordPress
- Extend your WordPress to make a proposal management service.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin files to the `/wp-content/plugins/asphalt-proposal-manager` directory, or install the plugin through the WordPress plugins screen directly.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' screen in WordPress.
3. Configure the plugin in the settings.

= WordPress Admin Method =  
 1. Go to your administration area in WordPress `Plugins > Add`  
 2. Look for `Proposal Crafter` (use search form)  
 3. Click on Install and activate the plugin

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is this plugin free? =

Yes, it is free under the GPLv3 license.

= Can I use this plugin with my current theme? = 
Yes, Proposal Crafter will work with any kind of theme.

= Can I customize the appearance of my proposals (e.g., add logos, change colors)? =  
Yes! You can modify templates using the built-in Gutenberg editor. Changing color or logos or even adding other elements is possible.

= Does this plugin support e-signatures? =  
Yes, clients can digitally sign proposals (upload signed Image or directly Type), making approval seamless.   

= Are notifications sent when a client views or accepts a proposal? = 
Yes, notifications are sent to you whenever a client opens/views/accepts/declines a proposal. It's a pro feature of this plugin.

= Are proposals stored securely on my server or third-party services used for storage? =
All your proposal data is stored in your WordPress. You own every piece of information on your proposals/clients. No need to pay to keep/access your data.
